MAKE YOURSELF AT HOME

A Mac. Your items.
A few setup steps.

Download the Mac app, drag TagTracker into Applications, and open it there. The installer disk ejects automatically. Choose your items, allow the macOS permissions, and start tracking. Signed updates are delivered with Sparkle.

Read the setup and source guide ↗
Start with the source
git clone https://github.com/danielyaa5/tagtracker.git
cd tagtracker
make build
python3 scripts/setup.py --item 'Keys' --item 'Backpack'
  1. Choose your exact Find My item names.
  2. Grant Accessibility and Screen Recording permissions.
  3. Load the local services, then open the dashboard.

The setup guide includes the service commands and removal instructions.

◎

A record of what was shown.

TagTracker reads Find My's visible interface. A saved observation can contain an older location, and gaps are possible. The Mac must stay awake, logged in, and unlocked. Find My opens during a scan.

Map pins use your saved coordinates or approximate town centers. They aren't GPS fixes or measured routes. History stays local; OpenStreetMap receives requests for the map tiles you view.
